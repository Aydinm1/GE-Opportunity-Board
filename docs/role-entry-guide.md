# Role entry guide

Use this guidance as descriptions/help text in the Airtable role intake form. It is for people publishing roles, not applicants completing an application. Formula fields should remain read-only and use their source inputs.

| Field | What the role author should enter | Example / rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role title source | A concise role name. The displayed title formula adds programme/team information. | `Content Creation Lead`; do not repeat the programme name in the source title. |
| Programme / Functional Area | The programme/office that owns this role. | Choose the correct existing option. |
| Team/Vertical | The specific team within the programme, if applicable. | `Content Development Team`; do not put geography or work mode here. |
| Location / Base | A short place or geographical eligibility restriction. | `Mombasa, Kenya`, `Remote`, or `Remote — Kenya-based applicants`. Put travel details in role description/responsibilities. |
| Work Type | The arrangement for performing this role. | Choose `Remote`, `Hybrid`, or `Onsite`, including for remote roles with a geographical restriction. |
| Role Type | Whether the position is volunteer or paid. | Confirm with the team whether this should be public; existing field description and current app disagree. |
| Start Date | Intended start date; confirm it is still accurate before publishing. | A date, not application deadline. |
| Duration source inputs | Intended start/end or duration inputs used by the duration formulas. | Short programmes require accurate dates; do not type explanatory text into derived duration/category fields. |
| Displayed Purpose of the Role | Two or three complete sentences explaining why the role exists and what contribution it makes. | A short summary, not a duplicate title or a list of requirements. |
| Key Responsibilities | Five to seven complete, action-oriented bullets, each on its own line. | `Coordinate programme teams and maintain the monthly content calendar.` Commas within a sentence are normal; never use them to separate bullets. Avoid standalone category headings. |
| 10. Required Qualifications | Select qualifications that an applicant must possess. | Selecting `Other` requires actual details in Other Required Qualifications. For digital tools, name the relevant tools in custom details. |
| Other Required Qualifications | The specific additional must-have education, experience, tools or skills, one per line. | `Experience creating social media assets in Canva.` Do not enter `Other`, `Specify below`, or instructions for the next person. |
| Preferred Qualifications | Select helpful qualifications that are not mandatory. | Do not put a must-have here. Specify particular systems or language requirements in real text rather than leaving the intake prompt visible. |
| Point form of essential skills, education & experience | **Requires owner clarification:** the app currently treats this as extra preferred qualifications despite the word essential. | Until clarified, place must-haves in Other Required Qualifications. A separate preferred-details field or a renamed intake label may be appropriate after agreement. |
| Displayed Estimated Time Commitment | Weekly hours expected for this role. | Choose the appropriate supported range; never put hours in Languages Required. Put variation/travel caveats in the role description or TOR. |
| Languages Required | Required languages and proficiency; one complete requirement per line. | `English — fluent written and spoken`. No weekly hours or unrelated qualifications. |
| TOR Upload | Upload the final applicant-facing document(s). | Use clear filenames; uploads take priority over TOR links. |
| TOR | A valid applicant-accessible HTTP/HTTPS document link when not uploading files. | Verify it opens without an internal account or permission request. |
| Role status / Published? | Publish only after reviewing completeness and accuracy. Published? is derived from role status. | Confirm duplicate-looking roles are distinct before marking them actively recruiting. |

## Before publishing

- Check the applicant-facing page and TOR agree on duties, requirements, location, hours and dates.
- Supply overview, responsibilities, required qualifications, work type/location, language requirements, start timing and weekly hours where applicable. Resolve missing information with the role owner rather than guessing.
- If Other is selected, include the actual qualification. Replace all intake instructions with substantive content.
- Keep lists as one complete item per line. Preserve ordinary commas within each item.
- Distinguish similarly named roles, or unpublish accidental duplicates after owner approval.
- Confirm required versus preferred criteria explicitly. Do not move an essential criterion into preferred just because a field is ambiguous.
